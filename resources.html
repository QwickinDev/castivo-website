Frequently asked

Common questions,
straight answers.

What is Castivo, exactly?
A cloud digital signage platform plus an Android TV app. You design content in your browser, the app on your TV plays it. We're the brain — your TV is the screen.
Do you supply the TV or hardware?
No. Castivo is software-only. You bring your own Android TV. If you don't have one, a $79 Chromecast with Google TV plugged into any HDMI display works perfectly.
How does the pairing work?
Install the Castivo app on your Android TV. The app shows a 6-digit passcode on screen. Enter that code into your account in the cloud portal. Your TV is now linked to your account. The whole process takes under 60 seconds.
What if my internet drops?
The Castivo app caches your scheduled content locally on the Android TV. If the internet drops, the screen keeps playing the cached content. As soon as connection returns, it syncs any updates. You won't see a black screen.
Can I run different content on different screens?
Yes. On the Pro plan and above, you can group screens by location, tag, or campaign and push different content to each group. You can also override per-screen if needed.
Is my data hosted in Australia?
Yes. Castivo's cloud platform is hosted in Australian data centres. Your content, account data, and analytics never leave Australian jurisdiction.
What support do you offer?
Free and Starter: email support during business hours. Pro: priority email and chat. Business: phone support and a dedicated account manager. All plans include access to our help centre and setup guides.
Will Castivo come and set it up for me?
Yes — for Pro and Business customers in metro Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, Perth, and Adelaide, in-person setup is included free. Outside metro, we run a 30-minute remote setup call instead.
How do I cancel?
After your initial term, log in, click Account, click Cancel, and we'll process it within 30 days' notice. No 70% penalty, no exit fees, no last-minute negotiation.
Can I get my content back if I leave?
Yes. Email support and we'll provide a complete export of your content and account data within 30 days at no charge.
