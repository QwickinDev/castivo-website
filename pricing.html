Pricing FAQ

Common questions,
straight answers.

Do I need to buy hardware from Castivo?
No. Castivo is software-only. You bring your own Android TV — any modern model works (Android 8.0 or later, 2GB RAM, 1080p). We'll publish a list of tested models, or just ask us before you buy.
How does the free plan work?
One screen, free forever, no credit card needed. The screen displays a small Castivo watermark — that's the only limitation. Upgrade anytime to remove it and add unlimited screens.
What's the difference between monthly and annual billing?
Monthly is simpler — pay as you go, cancel anytime. Annual saves you about 17% in exchange for a single yearly invoice. You can switch between them at any renewal.
Can I cancel any time?
Yes. After your initial term (month-to-month, 12, or 24 months — your choice), you can cancel with 30 days' notice and pay nothing extra. No 70% early-termination penalty like the enterprise providers.
Do you offer custom pricing for very large deployments?
Yes. Volume discounts kick in automatically at 10 screens (20% off) and 25 screens (30% off). For 50+ screens or white-label requirements, contact us for a custom quote.
What payment methods do you accept?
Credit card (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) or direct debit (BECS) — your choice. We process payments via Stripe Australia. All invoices are AUD with GST itemised.
Will the price ever go up?
If we adjust prices, we'll cap any increase at CPI + 3%, no more than once per 12 months, and give you 60 days' written notice. You can cancel within those 60 days at no cost if you don't want to accept the change.
