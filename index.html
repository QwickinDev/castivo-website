How it works

From box to on-air in 60 seconds.

No technicians. No installers. No screens to ship. Just download the app, enter a passcode, and you're broadcasting.

01 — SIGN UP

Pick your plan

Sign up at castivo.com.au, choose a tier, pay by card or direct debit. Account live immediately — no sales call required.

02 — INSTALL

Drop the app on your TV

Install Castivo on your existing Android TV. We'll walk you through it remotely, or come to you in person if you're in metro Australia.

03 — PAIR

Enter the 6-digit code

The app shows a one-time passcode on the TV. Type it into the cloud portal — done. Pairing takes under 60 seconds.

04 — PUBLISH

Push your content live

Upload images, video, or build in the editor. Hit publish — content appears on the screen seconds later. Update from anywhere.
