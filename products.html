Two parts.
One screen.

Castivo is a cloud content management platform paired with a rock-solid Android TV app. The cloud is where you build and schedule. The TV is where it shows up. Pairing takes 60 seconds.

PART ONE

The cloud platform

A web-based content editor where you design layouts, schedule content, and manage every screen across every location. Open it in any browser, on any device.

  • → Drag-and-drop content editor
  • → 500+ industry templates
  • → Schedule by date, time, location
  • → Multi-screen group control
  • → Live data integrations
PART TWO

The Android TV app

A lightweight app that runs on your existing Android TV. Sideload it, enter a 6-digit passcode, and it's paired. Then it just plays — quietly, reliably, forever.

  • → Runs on Android TV 8.0+
  • → Auto-recovers after power loss
  • → Caches content for offline play
  • → Auto-updates silently overnight
  • → One-time-passcode pairing

Scheduling

Every screen.

Group screens by store, city, or region. Push national content in a click, or run hyper-local content per site. See every screen's status at a glance.

On your Android TV

Built for Android TV.
Tested everywhere.

The Castivo app runs on any modern Android TV — no media player needed. Bring your own screen, or grab a $79 Chromecast and you're done.

Sideload in 2 minutes

Direct download from castivo.com.au or sideload via USB. We've documented the process for every common Android TV model.

Auto-recovery

Power loss? Internet drop? The app boots back up the second the TV turns on. Plays cached content if offline.

Smart caching

Content is downloaded once and cached locally. Plays at full quality even on slow connections. Updates only what's changed.

Auto-updates

The app updates itself silently overnight. Bug fixes, security patches, new features — you never have to touch the TV again.

One-time passcode

The app shows a 6-digit code. Enter it once in the cloud portal — done. No accounts, no logins on the TV, no fuss.

Compatibility list

Tested on Hisense, TCL, Sony Bravia, Chromecast with Google TV, Nvidia Shield, generic Android TV boxes, and more.

MINIMUM SPEC

What your Android TV needs

Almost every Android TV sold in Australia in the last 5 years works. If you're buying new, anything in the $300+ range is rock-solid.

OS: Android TV 8.0+
RAM: 2 GB minimum
Display: 1080p (4K supported)
Internet: 5 Mbps down / 1 Mbps up
